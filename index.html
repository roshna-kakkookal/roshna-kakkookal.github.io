Research Profile

My doctoral research investigates the early and late Universe using observations of the cosmic microwave background (CMB) and Type Ia supernovae. My work focuses on early-Universe cosmology- inflation and Loop Quantum Cosmology (LQC), primordial non-Gaussianity, dark energy, and cosmological parameter estimation using CMB and Type Ia supernova observations.

PhD in Physics, NITK Surathkal — Thesis submitted; awaiting viva/completion.

Research guide: Dr. V. Sreenath , NITK Surathkal

Research Interests

Early and late Universe, primordial non-Gaussianity, cosmic microwave background, type Ia supernovae, dark energy- ΛCDM and extensions.

Research Methods

Likelihood-based analysis, Bayesian and frequentist inference, Markov chain Monte Carlo, nested sampling, Bootstrap and Jackknife resampling techniques.
